## Goal

Design a manually powered machine that peels carrots along their full length while remaining safe, compact, and easy to clean.

## Highlights

- Compared mechanism concepts against the design requirements.
- Developed the lever-driven mechanism and CAD assembly.
- Sized the transmission, shafts, bearings, and fasteners, with manufacturing drawings and assembly tolerances.
