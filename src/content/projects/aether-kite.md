## Goal

Develop an _optimized_ plug-and-play system to tow boats using a kite in order to reduce their energy consumption. See more [here](https://aetherswisskite.ch/).

## Highlights

- Designed, built, and integrated a kite position sensor.
- Collected data and implemented extended and unscented Kalman filters for localization.
- Led a six-person control team working on data acquisition, system identification, simulation and control.
